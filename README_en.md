# English Listening & Speaking Assistant — Android Closed Testing Recruitment

We are preparing to release an Android app called **English Listening & Speaking Assistant**, designed to help students prepare for the English listening and speaking section of China’s National College Entrance Examination (Gaokao).

We are now looking for real Android users to join our Google Play closed testing program and help us complete real-device testing before release.

## About the App

English Listening & Speaking Assistant is designed for students and families preparing for the Gaokao English listening and speaking exam.

The app currently includes training features such as:

* Part A: Imitative Reading
* Part B: Role Play
* Part C: Story Retelling
* Mock Exams
* Intelligent Scoring
* Mistake Review and Practice

For the Android version, we are currently focusing on testing the mobile training experience, microphone recording, listening and reading playback, speech recognition and scoring, and authorization/license verification.

## Requirements

To participate, you should:

* Have an Android phone or tablet.
* Have a Google/Gmail account that can access Google Play normally.
* Be willing to join the Google Play closed test and remain enrolled for at least 14 days.
* Be able to install and open the app and complete a basic Part A imitative reading test.

## How to Join

1. Send your Gmail address to the developer so it can be added to the Google Play closed testing list.

   Contact email: **[Ericyang81@gmail.com](mailto:Ericyang81@gmail.com)**

   Suggested email subject:

   **Request to Join English Listening & Speaking Assistant Android Closed Test**

2. After your Gmail account has been added to the tester list, open the following Google Play testing link:

   https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tianyuanpro.englishspeaking

3. Choose to join the testing program.

4. After successfully joining, install the app from Google Play:

   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tianyuanpro.englishspeaking

5. Open the app and complete a basic test session.

> Note: Google Play requires at least 12 testers to remain enrolled in the closed test for 14 consecutive days. Please do not leave the testing program early once you have joined.

## Suggested Testing Areas

You can help us check the following:

* Whether the app installs and launches correctly.
* Whether the home screen and Part A imitative reading page display properly.
* Whether listening and reading audio playback works correctly.
* Whether microphone permission can be granted normally.
* Whether recording, stopping, and scoring feedback work smoothly.
* Whether authorization status checks and limited-access messages are clear and understandable.

## Reporting Problems

You can report issues through GitHub Issues or by email.

When reporting a problem, it would be helpful to include:

* Phone or tablet model
* Android version
* The page or screen where the issue occurred
* Steps to reproduce the problem
* Screenshots or screen recordings, if available

For GitHub Issues, please click the **Issues** tab at the top of this repository.

## Privacy Notice

Please do not post sensitive personal information in public GitHub Issues, including your license information, phone number, ID number, or home address.

If you need to provide your Gmail address or any authorization-related information, please send it to the developer by email instead.

Thank you for helping us test the Android version of English Listening & Speaking Assistant.

Your feedback will be used directly to improve the learning and practice experience before release.
